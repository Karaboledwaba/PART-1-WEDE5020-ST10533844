About FlowTech Plumbers

Established in Johannesburg in 2018, FlowTech Plumbers began as a family-run business with a single goal: to provide honest, reliable plumbing solutions to our community. Over the years, we have grown into a leading residential trade contractor known for exceptional craftsmanship.

Our Purpose

Our Mission

To provide Johannesburg homeowners with transparent, expert plumbing services that prioritize long-term reliability over quick fixes.

Our Vision

To be the premier plumbing partner in South Africa, recognized for setting the standard in trade integrity and customer-centric service.

Our Core Values

  • Integrity: We provide transparent pricing with no hidden costs.
  • Excellence: Every repair is executed with meticulous attention to detail.
  • Reliability: When you have an emergency, we arrive on time, every time.
